Burst Super-Res merge a burst of photos into one 2× image

Drop 2–8 photos of the same scene

JPEG or PNG, all the same size

    What to expect. The output is exactly twice the width and height of your photos. Merging cuts noise and cleans up fine detail a little. It does not give you four times the detail. In our tests (see README) the gain over upscaling one photo was about 0.7–1.3 dB PSNR.

    Needs a static scene and a tripod or a very steady hand. Small shifts and slight rotation are corrected. Moving subjects, parallax and camera shake blur are not.
